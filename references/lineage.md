# Lineage and novelty boundary

## Closest methodological predecessors

- **Stevens, Myers, and Constantine, “Structured Design” (1974)** — cohesion and coupling provide the module-quality axis. Coincidental or merely logical cohesion explains residual and “cross-cutting” buckets; functional cohesion supplies the positive ideal. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1147/sj.132.0115)
- **David Parnas, “On the Criteria To Be Used in Decomposing Systems into Modules” (1972)** — hide design decisions likely to change; modularize by what varies together, not surface workflow alone. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/361598.361623)
- **Herbert Simon, “The Architecture of Complexity” (1962)** — near-decomposability: strong interactions within modules and weaker interactions between them. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/1886091)
- **Christopher Alexander, _Notes on the Synthesis of Form_ (1964)** — hierarchical decomposition by natural cleavages in interacting requirements. “A City Is Not a Tree” (1965) supplies the corrective: many living structures are semi-lattices, so a tree must disclose load-bearing overlaps.
- **Donald Steward, “The Design Structure System” (1981)** — Design Structure Matrices expose dependencies and support clustering and sequencing. They are the natural alternative when explicit dependency data is stronger than semantic evidence. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSMC.1981.4308571)
- **Glaser and Strauss, _The Discovery of Grounded Theory_ (1967)** — iterative coding is an adjacent multi-pass method: generate candidate categories, relate them, then consolidate around a coherent account.

## Supporting lineage

- George Miller's 1956 channel-capacity work motivates limited branching, but does not justify forcing every cut to seven or treating 3–7 as a law. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0043158)
- Baldwin and Clark's _Design Rules_ (2000) frames modules through stable interfaces and substitutability.
- Graph community detection and modularity metrics offer algorithmic alternatives when the object already has a trustworthy interaction graph; their resolution limits make them insufficient as the sole oracle for small semantic corpora.

## Reuse posture

**Compose: extract and cite.** The criteria and decomposition problem are established prior art. The agent-operable combination here remains useful because it adds:

- graded light, medium, and heavy procedures;
- decoration prefiltering before candidate generation;
- separation of organizing-principle search from child-count search;
- one-ply semantic lookahead and an explicit kind-homogeneity probe;
- mandatory Residuals and semi-lattice disclosure;
- a stable JSON contract for dependent skills.

No equivalent installed skill supplies this whole operation. `surface` remains a consumer because it adds workframe sourcing, developmental modes, regeneration, and its own Markdown format.
